## Usage policy

- No generative AI photos are used in this collection.
- Licensed Unsplash photographs are **illustrations**, **not** DCG's premises, equipment, staff, customers or completed projects.
- Each of the five Unsplash pages was verified as **“Free to use under the Unsplash License”** (standard free license, not Unsplash+). License: https://unsplash.com/license — commercial use is permitted **without required on-page attribution**. For a cleaner interface, no optional photographer-credit strips are shown; authors and source links remain archived in this manifest.
- These five photos are genuine photography, but photo authenticity cannot be independently guaranteed beyond the original photographers' source pages. Do not claim their subjects are DCG personnel.
- Downloaded JPG originals (`*-licensed-source.jpg`) are preserved unchanged in this repository; WebP files are derived only by resize/compression, without filters, gradients, overlays, facial retouching or object insertion.
- **Event photos** and the approved founder portrait are kept byte-for-byte and are never cropped.
- The original five `public/assets/hero-*.webp` files remain as rollback artifacts only, no longer used in page markup. Their origin/rights were not established and must not be republished without review.
- Images and logo files have separate provenance categories; logos and their source artwork are never replaced by photographs.

## Photo sourcing and content checks

The source IDs are pinned in `tools/import_editorial_photos.py` and `tools/import_talent_licensed_photo.py` and the original source JPEGs are preserved. All replacements should be reviewed against this registry before merging, with browser checks at 390/768/834/1024/1440px, image decoded dimensions and actual URL access. Do not substitute `source.unsplash.com` random image endpoints, Unsplash+ content, or unlicensed Google/blog/reposted images.
